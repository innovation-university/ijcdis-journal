Planned process

From submission to publication

1. Submit

Authors upload an anonymous manuscript, title page, declarations, and supplementary files through the approved system.

2. Review

Editorial checks and independent peer review are recorded in the system with controlled access and conflict management.

3. Publish

Accepted files undergo production, metadata validation, proof approval, licence display, and permanent publication.
