Volume 1, Issue 1 — planned for 2027

No articles have been published by ICSIS at this stage. The issue record will appear only after manuscripts complete independent peer review, production, author proof approval, and formal publication.

Publication threshold

The editorial team is planning a coherent first issue. Quality, research integrity, and complete peer review take precedence over an article-count target.

Permanent record

Published article pages should include title, authors, affiliations, abstract, keywords, dates, licence, citation, declarations, references, and stable file links.

Archive

Past volumes and issues will be browsable by year and issue after publication, with a documented preservation and backup arrangement.

No placeholder publications: working drafts, generated manuscripts, fabricated data, or papers lacking genuine authorship and review will not be used to create an artificial publication history.
