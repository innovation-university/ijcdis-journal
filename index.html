Quality framework

Planned controls for the scholarly record

Editorial decisions are intended to be based on fit, originality, methodological soundness, transparent reporting, research ethics, and independent review—not on payment or institutional affiliation.

Independent peer review

Research content will undergo editorial screening and, when suitable, double-blind review by at least two independent experts.

Research integrity

Policies cover authorship, conflicts, data and code, ethics approvals, AI disclosure, corrections, retractions, complaints and appeals.

Open research

The proposed model is immediate open access under CC BY 4.0, with authors retaining copyright, subject to publisher approval.
