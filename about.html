About the journal

Mission, scope, readership, article types, and the proposed publication model.

Status: IJCDIS is a new journal under institutional development. The inaugural issue is planned for 2027, subject to formal approval and operational readiness.

Mission

IJCDIS aims to publish rigorous, reproducible, and ethically conducted research that advances computer science and information systems and connects methodological innovation with practical impact.

Aims and scope

The journal considers work that makes a clear scholarly contribution in:

  • Artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, trustworthy AI, and computational intelligence.
  • Data science, data mining, big-data engineering, knowledge discovery, and decision support.
  • Information systems, digital transformation, e-government, enterprise systems, and human–computer interaction.
  • Cybersecurity, privacy, cryptography, network security, digital forensics, and blockchain applications.
  • Cloud, edge and high-performance computing; Internet of Things; embedded and distributed systems.
  • Software engineering, software quality, requirements engineering, testing, and DevOps.
  • Computer vision, natural language processing, multimedia, and responsible domain applications.

Readership

Researchers, postgraduate students, academic staff, research engineers, data scientists, information-systems professionals, and policy or industry practitioners who use computing research in real-world settings.

Article types

  • Original Research Article
  • Systematic or Scoping Review
  • Survey or Tutorial Article
  • Dataset, Software or Benchmark Paper
  • Applied Case Study
  • Short Communication, when the contribution is complete and verifiable
