For authors

Pre-launch manuscript preparation guidance and proposed editorial requirements.

Submission is not yet open. This page supports manuscript preparation only. Do not send confidential data or sensitive material through informal channels.

Before submission

  • The work must be original, not under review elsewhere, and not substantially duplicate another publication.
  • All authors must meet authorship criteria, approve the manuscript, and accept accountability.
  • Disclose funding, conflicts, related manuscripts, preprints, and material use of generative AI tools.
  • Obtain required ethics approval, consent, data permission, and institutional authorization before submission.

Manuscript structure

Include a title page, a 200–250-word structured abstract, 5–7 keywords, Introduction, Related Work, Methods, Results, Discussion, Limitations, Conclusion, and complete end-matter statements.

End matter should address CRediT author contributions, funding, ethics, consent, conflicts of interest, data and code availability, acknowledgements, and references.

Formatting

  • Prepare the manuscript in Microsoft Word or LaTeX.
  • Use IEEE numbered citations in order of appearance.
  • Keep equations editable and define all symbols.
  • Submit editable tables and publication-quality figures; do not use screenshots of tables.
  • Report software versions, hardware, random seeds, data splits, metrics, baselines, and statistical procedures sufficiently for reproduction.

Data, code and reproducibility

Every article must include a Data Availability Statement. Deposit non-sensitive data and code in a trusted repository where legally and ethically possible; explain justified restrictions.

Generative AI

AI systems cannot be authors. Authors remain responsible for accuracy, originality, citations, confidentiality, and intellectual property. Material use of generative AI in writing, coding, image generation, analysis, or translation must be disclosed with the tool, purpose, and human verification.

Copyright and licence

Proposed policy: authors retain copyright and grant ICSIS first-publication rights. Articles would be published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0), subject to institutional approval.

Fees

No submission fee or article processing charge is proposed for the launch phase. Any future fee and waiver policy must be approved and displayed before submission and must not influence editorial decisions.

Submission checklist

  • Anonymous manuscript for double-blind review.
  • Separate title page with author details.
  • Editable figures, tables, and supplementary files.
  • All ethics, conflict, funding, contribution, data/code, and AI-use statements.
  • Cover letter explaining contribution, scope fit, and related submissions.
