Publication ethics

Proposed integrity policies protecting participants, authors, reviewers, editors, and the scholarly record.

Authorship and contributorship

Authorship requires a substantial scholarly contribution, participation in drafting or critical revision, approval of the final version, and accountability. Contributions should be described using the CRediT taxonomy. Gift, guest, and ghost authorship are not acceptable.

Human and animal research

Studies involving people, identifiable or clinical data, or interventions must state the approving ethics body, approval identifier, and consent process where applicable. Animal studies must state approval and welfare standards.

Conflicts and funding

Authors, reviewers, and editors must disclose interests that could reasonably affect—or appear to affect—the work. Funding sources and the funder’s role must be reported.

Data and image integrity

Fabrication, falsification, selective omission, misleading manipulation, and inappropriate image alteration are prohibited. Editors may request raw data, code, logs, or original images to clarify integrity concerns.

Originality and overlap

Similarity software may support screening, but no single percentage automatically proves originality or misconduct. Editors assess the source, location, amount, and nature of overlap, including duplicated methods, recycled text, redundant publication, and plagiarism.

AI-assisted work

Authors must disclose material generative-AI use and remain fully accountable. Confidential manuscripts, reviews, personal data, or proprietary material must not be uploaded to external systems without authorization and suitable safeguards.

Corrections and retractions

Errors affecting interpretation or the record will be corrected transparently. Serious unreliability or misconduct may require retraction, expression of concern, or another linked notice. Published notices should preserve the integrity and traceability of the record.

Complaints and appeals

Appeals must identify a specific factual, procedural, or ethical concern. Complaints will be handled independently from the person whose conduct or decision is challenged whenever possible.

Allegations

Allegations are assessed fairly, confidentially, and without retaliation. The journal may contact authors, institutions, ethics bodies, publishers, or other appropriate parties when evidence warrants escalation.
